Muhurtham


Muhurat marks the auspicious time for wedding rituals, ensuring divine blessings, prosperity, and a harmonious beginning for the couple. As wedding planners, for the early morning "Muhurat" or "Murtham" ceremony, we ensure everything is prepared with precision. We coordinate the setup of the sacred space, arranging floral decorations, altars, and idols according to tradition. We ensure proper seating for family members and priests, manage the timing for rituals, and provide offerings like flowers, fruits, and sweets. Our goal is to create a serene, auspicious environment for the couple to begin their wedding day with blessings.
