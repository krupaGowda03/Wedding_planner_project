Haldi



The Haladí ceremony is a traditional pre-wedding ritual in many South Asian cultures, especially in India. It involves applying a turmeric paste to the bride and groom's skin to purify and beautify them before the wedding.As wedding planners, we meticulously prepare for the Haldi ceremony by sourcing pure turmeric powder, ensuring its authenticity and quality. We arrange traditional vessels for the turmeric paste, coordinate with decorators to create a vibrant yellow-themed ambiance, and organize seating for guests. Additionally, we manage the timing of the ceremony and oversee the application of the turmeric paste to the bride and groom, ensuring a smooth and culturally rich experience.
