Sangeet


The Sangeet celebration in weddings is a lively pre-wedding tradition, typically filled with music, dance, and joyful festivities. It brings families and friends together to celebrate the union of the couple. As wedding planners, we organize and coordinate every aspect of the Sangeet, from selecting a suitable venue to arranging music, dance performances, and lighting. We collaborate with choreographers, caterers, and decorators to ensure a vibrant atmosphere, making sure everything flows smoothly, creating a memorable celebration for the couple and guests.
